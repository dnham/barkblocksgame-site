Need help?

Email bdunham54@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Where did my progress go? Progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it.

Why do I see ads? Ads keep Bark Blocks free. Optional ads give you rewards like extra bones or a second chance.

Can I turn off personalized ads? Yes. Go to iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Does it work without internet? Yes. You can play offline; only ads and rewards from ads need a connection.
