Bark Blocks

The cozy block puzzle where every block is a puppy, and one of them is yours.

Download on theApp Store Free to play · Works offline

How it plays

Drag pups. Clear lines. Wag tails.

Drop puppy blocks onto the 8×8 board and fill a row or column to clear it. No timers and no pressure, just you and your dog.

Create your own pup

Name your dog, pick a style, and choose its fur, ear and muzzle colors. Have a real dog at home? Match its colors from a photo and watch your best friend show up on the board.

Hit the Purebred Jackpot

Clearing lines with your own pup's blocks scores double. Complete a whole line of just your dog and the entire board fills with your pup and blasts away.

8 superpowers and a full wardrobe

Charge your power meter, then tap your pup to unleash Fire Hose, Avalanche, Cannonball and more. Earn bones as you play and spend them on party hats, crowns, sunglasses and bow ties.
